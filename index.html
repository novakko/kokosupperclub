About Us

Slow food in a
sleepy village

Nestled in a small seaside village where time moves differently, SupperClub is our answer to fast dining and disconnected meals. Once a month, we open our home—an old fisherman's cottage overlooking the bay—to twelve strangers.

The menu follows the rhythm of the seasons. In autumn, wild mushrooms from the coastal woods. Winter brings root vegetables and hearty stews. Spring explodes with tender greens, and summer offers the day's catch grilled over charcoal.

"The best meals are not about the food, but about the gathering."
